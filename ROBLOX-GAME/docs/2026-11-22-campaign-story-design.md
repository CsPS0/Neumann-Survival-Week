# The Empty School on Roblox: the story day, second version (design)

Date: 2026-11-22. Status: approved by the author ("Yes, build it"). Changes the middle and the night of
`2026-11-20-campaign-design.md`; everything not named here stays as it was (lessons, quizzes, clues, the porter's keys,
the quest items, the endings vote, difficulty).

## The day

| Step | Game clock | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Introduction | 06:30 | A title card ("The Empty School. Neumann Survival Week, day one.") and the timetable on the phone. |
| Classes | 07:30- | As before: seated paper tests, breaks with clues and teachers. |
| Explosion | end of the last lesson | Lab 14 (chemistry) explodes: flash, bang, alarm, the screen goes white. |
| Time slip | +20 minutes | Everyone wakes on the Aula floor 20 minutes later. Nobody remembers. The school is darker and foggier, spider webs grow in the corners, students and teachers cough. The possessed teacher is the only one who is not sick (a clue). |
| Sent home | 13:20-18:00 | A teacher announces that everyone goes home. The front door says "Go home". A security guard searches the school with a torch. |
| Night | 18:00-06:00 | The lights die and the exits are sealed with web. A scream from the Aula: the entity eats the guard (a short scene), then it attacks. The hunt, the last clues and the quest follow. |
| The altar | night | With the three items on the altar, the team names the entity (the vote that used to be at dusk). Right: the ritual starts. Wrong: ending 8. |
| 06:00 | | Not done: the morning staff find you in the webbed school. Ending 7, now "Expelled at dawn". |

## Choices

- **Go home** (the front door in the afternoon, or caught by the guard, who walks you out): you are taken to your room
  at home. The clock runs on. At 18:00 your phone lights up: a Neugram post of the school door covered in webs, and
  the guard is not answering. In your room:
  - **Go back to the school**: you climb in through a broken window at the far end of the school (by day you still
    have to hide from the guard; at night you are in the hunt).
  - **Go to sleep**: you are out of the run ("went home"). If everyone ends that way the run ends with the new
    ending 10, "Safe at home".
- **Stay**: hide from the guard (lockers, toilets, empty rooms) until night falls.

## Being caught at night

Every catch at night is the grab from Hide and Seek (`Grab.luau`): the entity holds you, you mash the defend key
(X) to throw it off; too slow and it takes your arm, an eye, then eats you (out, "caught"). A teammate can pull you
free (hold E 3 s, -25 health). Easy, alone: being eaten wakes you at the entrance with 20 game minutes lost, as
before. The guard's scene ends with the entity attacking the nearest player the same way.

## Rulings

- The front door: before the slip "Leave the school (you are out)" as before; after it "Go home"; at night sealed
  ("The web will not tear"). The web over the doors dissolves when the ritual is done.
- The guard is the old caretaker with a new name and look. He now sends you home instead of expelling you, and he
  patrols on every difficulty (slower and shorter-sighted on Easy). He leaves the patrol at 18:00 for his scene.
- If nobody walks into the Aula within a minute of the scream, the scene plays unseen and the entity starts hunting.
- The scene stays at Moderate 13+: the guard is lifted and shaken, the lights flicker, a scream, his torch drops and
  rolls; no blood.
- Joining mid-run: before 18:00 as a student; after it as a spectator.
- Webs are built from thin parts (no uploaded images). They grow from the slip to the night and fill the doors at
  night.
