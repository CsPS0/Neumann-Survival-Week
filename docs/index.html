Four ways to play

Everyone on a server plays the same round. Pick the mode in the menu (the ` key).

Story day

1+ players

One school day from 06:30. Write tests at your desk, collect clues in the breaks. After the Lab 14 explosion twenty minutes go missing and the school changes. Ten endings.

Hide and Seek

1+ players

25 seconds to hide, four minutes to survive. The entity is the computer or one of you. Caught? Mash X, or a friend hits it from behind.

Social Deduction

3+ players

One of you is the entity and nobody knows who. Call a meeting with G, compare where everyone was, and vote someone out.

Free roam

1+ players

No entity, no clock. Walk the three floors, play ping pong in the Aula, shoot hoops in the gym, walk Csoki the dog.

Default keys

Everything sits under the left hand. Change any key in Menu, Settings, Controls.

WASD
Move (mouse: look)
E
Doors, lockers, people, items
Shift
Sprint
C
Crouch, slide while running
F T
Phone light, flash burst (stuns the entity)
Q
Phone: map, notes, clues, timetable
Tab B
Open the bag, drop it for a friend
X
Fight back: mash it when grabbed
G
Call a meeting (Social Deduction)

Alpha notes

Playing with friends

Join the same server and you are in the same round. For a closed group, open Menu, Friends and lobbies, make a private lobby and share its five-letter code.

Known issues

  • Private lobby codes and invites have not been tried in the live game yet.
  • Players under 16 cannot join until the content questionnaire is done.
  • A Hide and Seek or Social Deduction round repeats until someone presses Back to lobby.

Found a bug? Open an issue on GitHub

Older versions

These are no longer developed or supported. Everything they had now lives in the Roblox game, so play that one.

The Empty School, Godot 4.7 Deprecated

The first 3D version: a five-day campaign, a roaming demon, local co-op and experimental VR. It still runs, but it gets no fixes.

Run it: install Godot 4.7, import HORROR-GAME/project.godot, press F5.

IKT: Survival Week, C# console Deprecated, unfinished

Where the project began: an ASCII art RPG with three scenes and a few conversations. Quests, scoring and the day system were never built, and it only runs on Windows.

Run it: install the .NET 10 SDK, then dotnet run in RPG-GAME/RPG-GAME.
